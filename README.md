# bladestorm

Horde-mode survival with a boomeranging plasma orb and Newtonian dogfighting.
Pilot a lone ship against an endless robot horde in a screen-wrapping arena,
zap enemies with a crackling energy orb up close, hurl it out as a wide
plasma globe that boomerangs back, and unleash a repel to scatter a crowd —
with a bullet-hell boss every 5 waves.
